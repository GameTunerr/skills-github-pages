---
title: "Madison Square Garden Rally"
date: 1939-02-20
---


Key Elements of the Rally:

• The Setting: The stage featured a massive 30-foot portrait of George Washington flanked by swastikas, as the Bund attempted to blend American patriotism with Nazi ideology.

• The Message: Speakers delivered anti-Semitic speeches, attacked President Franklin D. Roosevelt's New Deal, and promoted white supremacy.

• The Protests: Outside the arena, an estimated 100,000 anti-Nazi counter-protesters gathered. The NYPD deployed over 1,700 officers to maintain order, making it one of the largest police details in the city's history.
